New in 2.1.3

• Sharing from other apps works again: send a PDF or photo to Paperless 24 from the share sheet and the app opens and takes it. If the switch fails, nothing is lost — the file is picked up on the next launch.
• System search (Spotlight) finds documents again — and now covers the whole archive instead of just the page last loaded.
• Spotlight results show the document preview instead of the app icon.
• With several accounts, system search keeps them apart: a result never opens a document from another archive.
• "Rebuild Spotlight index" now reports how many documents made it into the index.

From 2.1.x

• Support for paperless-ngx 3.0: the app negotiates the API version per server and copes with dates that carry no time.
• Batch scanning: feed a whole stack at once and have the pages grouped into separate documents automatically.
• Ask your archive: put a question in your own words and get an answer with its sources.
• Drive a network scanner over Wi-Fi (AirScan/eSCL).
• Translate and summarise with no connection needed.
• Six colour themes, a free accent colour, OLED black and five alternative app icons.
• Home screen widget: latest documents or an overview.
• Three-column layout on iPad and Mac.
• Demo mode with a full set of sample data — try it without a server.
• Many small fixes to sorting, pagination and the offline queue.
